### 2. **Management Practices**
#### a. **Cover Crops (Green Manure)**
- **Leguminous Cover Crops**:
  - **Effectiveness**: Provide nitrogen to the soil and have nematode-suppressing properties.
  - **Examples**: Nemaguard, Nemaha.
  
- **Grass Cover Crops**:
  - **Effectiveness**: Improve soil physical properties, prevent nutrient loss, and suppress nematodes.
  - **Example**: Sudan Grass (Sorghum sudanense).
