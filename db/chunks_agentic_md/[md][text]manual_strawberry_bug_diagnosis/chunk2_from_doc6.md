### 1. **Nematodes in Strawberry Cultivation**
- **Root-Knot Nematodes**: These are parasitic worms that can cause significant damage to strawberries by feeding on the roots, leading to stunted growth and reduced yield.
- **Lesion Nematodes (Pratylenchus spp.)**: These nematodes attack the leaves and fruits of strawberry plants, causing deformities and reducing fruit quality.
